**Goal:** Land the second Phase-4 wave — the three non-pixi keyboard pages: **player** (the `/player` route AND the `/` root, old `player/index.tsx` 467 lines), **zen-keyboard** (110), **sheet-visualizer** (186) — as real Svelte pages replacing their stubs, plus everything they depend on: the `PlayerStore` / `PlayerControlsStore` / `ZenKeyboardStore` runes stores, the `VisualSong` model, the Player / ZenKeyboard / SheetVisualizer component trees, `SheetFrame`/`SheetFrame2`, slider + song-controls components, the page menus (hamburger snippet + `clickOutside` action + `MenuSidebar wrapperEl`), practice/approaching/play modes, note & audio recording, metronome UI, and the theme-page live-preview completion (Player half real, Composer half stays stubbed until 4c).
